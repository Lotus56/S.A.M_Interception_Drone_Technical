# Components

The electronics and other bought parts S.A.M needs, beside the case we
print ourselves ([parts/](parts/)). The list is split between what flies and
what stays on the ground.

## On the drone

| Component | Qty | What it does | Model | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Motors | 4 | Spin the propellers, one at the end of each arm | *to choose* | Planned |
| Propellers | 4 | Produce the lift and thrust | *to choose* | Planned |
| 6S battery | 1 | Powers everything on board: six lithium cells in series, 22.2 V nominal | *to choose* | Planned |
| ECU | 1 | Drives the motors from the flight computer's commands | *to choose* | Planned |
| Radio antenna | 1 | Links the drone to the ground station | *to choose* | Planned |
| Flight computer | 1 | Keeps the drone stable and runs the autopilot | *to choose* | Planned |

## Keeping this list up to date

When a part is chosen, fill in its model, ideally with a link to where it
was bought. Change its status as it moves along: **Planned**, **Ordered**,
**Arrived**, **Installed**.
